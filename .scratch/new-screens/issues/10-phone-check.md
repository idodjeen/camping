**What to build:** Ido runs Spec B's real-phone checklist on an installed app and the results are recorded in the handoff. Anything that fails becomes a fix ticket.

**Blocked by:** B02 to B09

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Chat list: a new message in a followed thread raises its count within one refresh, and opening the thread clears it
- [ ] Account: the phone notifications switch, including when the phone blocks it
- [ ] Guide: shown at a fresh test account's first sign-in, skippable, replayable
- [ ] New trip sheet: dates and group picker with the keyboard open
- [ ] No-access: sign in with an email in no group, copy it, switch account
- [ ] What's new: once after a deploy, not after a `[no-popup]` one
- [ ] Results recorded in the handoff
