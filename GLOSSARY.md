# מחנאות

A planning app for friend groups going on camping trips: who brings what, what to buy, what to eat
and who owes whom.

## People and places

**Group**:
A named circle of friends that holds people and their roles, and owns many trips.
_Avoid_: organization, team

**Trip**:
One outing of a group. All gear, meals, shopping, expenses and threads belong to a trip.
_Avoid_: event, camp

**Super admin**:
The one person who can see and manage every group.

**Group admin**:
A group member who manages that group's people and trips.
_Avoid_: owner, manager

**Editor**:
A group member who can add and change a trip's content.

**Viewer**:
A group member who can only read.
_Avoid_: guest

**Shopper**:
A trip member who ticks shopping items as bought, in that trip only.

## Conversations

**Thread** (שיחה):
The conversation attached to one gear, shopping or meal item.
_Avoid_: comments, discussion

**General chat** (צ׳אט כללי):
The one trip-wide conversation that is not attached to any item. It is a thread too.
_Avoid_: room

**Chat list** (רשימת השיחות):
All of a trip's threads, one row each, with the general chat pinned on top.
_Avoid_: chat feed, inbox

## Lists

**My gear** (אני מביא):
The gear items you took on, in full or in part.
_Avoid_: my list, my items

**Personal list** (הרשימה שלי):
Items only their owner sees, kept inside the gear screen.
_Avoid_: my gear (that is the gear you took on)

## Account

**Account** (חשבון):
A person's own page: profile, roles and notification settings.
_Avoid_: me, profile page
